# Feedback

Corrections, clarifications, and suggestions for this course site are welcome.

---

## What to report

| Category | Example |
|---|---|
| **Typo / error** | Wrong complexity bound, incorrect tree rotation, misspelled term |
| **Unclear explanation** | A step that doesn't follow, a definition that needs an example |
| **Broken code** | A snippet that doesn't compile or gives wrong output |
| **Broken link** | A reference or video URL that 404s |
| **Clarity on a tutorial problem** | Ambiguity in problem statement |

Grade queries, extensions, and other private matters → email the instructor directly.

---

## How to report

- **GitHub issue** on the course repository: `https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/ECEG-4192/issues`
- **Email**: subject `[ECEG-4192 feedback]`

---

## Response times

| Severity | Response | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Blocking** (site broken, wrong algorithm) | 24 h | 48 h |
| **Major** (wrong answer, unreadable example) | 3 days | 1 week |
| **Minor** (typo, unclear wording) | 1 week | next content pass |

Contributors who report substantive errors are acknowledged on the affected page (with permission).
